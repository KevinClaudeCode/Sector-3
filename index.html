Sector 3

MCC Sentinel Carrie, designation 03006

Common is dormant and most of the lights are out. Entities are grey, like the walls they move along. Your flashlight shows one direction at a time.

Stay alive. Scavenge instant noodles, Blue Water and Strikefish magazines. Magazines ride on Carrie's utility belt. Everything else goes in her bag, which has to be unzipped by hand before she can reach into it, and an open bag can lose things. The world doesn't stop while she digs through it.

The command room is your base. Seal its blast doors to keep entities out, and fix broken doors with steel alloy and a repair kit. Its water dispenser boils Blue Water for cooking noodles properly. Eating and drinking take time, so pick your moment.
